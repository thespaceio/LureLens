## The mark

Two Ls, one for **Lure** and one for **Lens**, locked into a frame you look through. Each L has an **s** for **SpaceIO** knocked out of its corner, like a maker's hallmark. The **lime ring** is the lens: the moment you inspect before you click.

## Type

- **Syne 800**: wordmark (always lowercase) and headlines
- **Inter**: body copy and buttons
- **JetBrains Mono**: sender addresses, links, timers, scores and labels

## Rules

- Keep one L stroke of clear space around the mark.
- Below 48 px use the bolder version without the "s" (the favicon already does).
- Never stretch, outline, recolour the lens coral or add glows.
